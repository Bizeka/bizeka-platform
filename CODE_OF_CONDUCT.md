# Code of conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, review the work rather than the person, and welcome questions and corrections. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and project-managed discussions. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close abusive discussions or restrict participation. Apply decisions consistently and allow people to explain or correct their behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer using their published GitHub contact information. Do not include private reports in public issues. A maintainer who is involved in a complaint should defer handling to another maintainer where possible. A reachable private contact must be verified before the repository is opened publicly.
